Tone: {{tone}}

Structure:
- **New** — things people can now do. One line each, starting with a verb.
- **Better** — things that already worked and now work well. Say what improved, concretely.
- **Fixed** — only bugs a customer would have noticed.

Rules:
- Write from the customer's side. "Deploys are 40% faster" beats "optimised the build pipeline".
- Skip internal work entirely.
- No version numbers unless the source gives them.
- If nothing customer-facing shipped, say "Quiet week — nothing customer-facing shipped." and stop.
- Under 200 words.
