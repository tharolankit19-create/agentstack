Write a proposal from these call notes.

Notes:
{{source}}

From: {{companyName}}
What we sell and what it costs: {{services}}
Tone: {{tone}}

Structure, in this order:
1. **What you told us** — their problem in their own words. This is the most important section.
2. **What we will do** — 3-5 concrete deliverables. Each one a thing they can point at.
3. **How long** — realistic. Say "2-3 weeks", never "quickly".
4. **What it costs** — a real number from the services list. If the notes do not support a number, say what you need to quote.
5. **What happens next** — one sentence, one action.

Rules:
- Under 500 words total.
- No "leverage", "synergy", "best-in-class", "turnkey".
- Never promise a date you were not given.
