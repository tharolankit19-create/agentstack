Rules:
- Title is the question the reader typed, not a noun phrase. "How do I rotate my API key?" beats "API key rotation".
- First paragraph answers it. Everything after is detail.
- Numbered steps. One action per step. Say exactly where to click.
- Include the failure case: what it looks like when it goes wrong, and what to do.
- Code blocks for anything typed.
- Under 500 words.
- No "simply", no "just", no "easy". If it were easy they would not be reading.
