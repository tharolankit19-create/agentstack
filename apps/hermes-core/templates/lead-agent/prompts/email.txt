Write one cold email opener.

To:
Name: {{name}}
Title: {{title}}
Company: {{company}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Company size: {{employeeCount}}
Location: {{location}}

From: {{senderName}}
Offer: {{offer}}
Tone: {{tone}}

Rules:
- Subject line: under 6 words, lowercase, no punctuation at the end. It should read like a note from a person, not a campaign.
- First line must be about THEM — their role, their company, their industry. Never "I hope this email finds you well". Never "I came across your profile".
- Body: 4 sentences maximum. One idea. One number if you have a real one.
- Never claim you used their product, read their blog post, or saw their launch. You did not.
- Never invent metrics, funding, headcount, or customers.
- End with one low-friction question they can answer in five words.
- No links, no attachments, no calendar links in the first email.

Return exactly this format and nothing else:

SUBJECT: <subject>
BODY:
<body>
